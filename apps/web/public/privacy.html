Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

This policy covers the Figbloom school-management apps — the parent, student and driver app ("Figbloom Schools"), and the teacher app ("Figbloom Teacher") — and the Figbloom web console they connect to (together, the "Service"). Figbloom Digital Group ("Figbloom", "we") builds and operates the Service on behalf of the schools that use it. Each school is the data controller for its own students', parents' and staff's information; Figbloom acts as the school's data processor, storing and transmitting that data on infrastructure Figbloom operates.

In short

  • Your school gives you an account. Most of what is in the Service was entered by your school, not by you.
  • We do not sell personal information and we do not show advertising. The apps contain no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting tools.
  • A driver's precise location is collected only during an active school-bus trip.
  • Fingerprint unlock is optional, and your fingerprint never leaves your phone.
  • Calls and live classes are not recorded by us.
  • To ask for your data to be corrected or removed, see how to request deletion.

Bloom (AI assistant)

Bloom helps school staff draft text (for example report card comments) and answer questions, and, where the school turns it on, helps learners study. A school can switch Bloom off, and the learner study helper is off unless the school turns it on (and may require a parent's opt-in).

  • What is sent to the AI provider is only what the request needs. Report card drafting, for example, sends a first name and results, never an admission number or surname. What a person types into a Bloom chat, and any file they attach to it, is sent too, so staff and learners are told not to put personal details in.
  • We keep a usage record for each request (who, which task, how much was used) but not the question or the answer. Staff can keep their own chat history in the Service. Files attached to a Bloom chat are read and not stored; a paper sent for importing is deleted once it has been read.
  • When a school and a person allow it, Bloom chat can search the web; learner and family details are not put into a search.

How we use this information

  • To run the features of the Service: attendance, gradebook, report cards, messaging, calls and live classes, fee payment, timetables, medical and visitor records, e-signatures, and — for the driver app — live bus-location sharing during a trip.
  • To deliver notifications you or your school have chosen.
  • To authenticate users and keep each school's data separate from every other school's.
  • To maintain and secure the Service, including diagnosing technical issues and preventing abuse.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for advertising or to build advertising profiles.

Who can see what

Data is scoped to the school it belongs to. Within a school: a parent sees their own children's records; a learner sees their own; a teacher sees the classes and learners they're assigned to; a driver sees the trips they're assigned to and the location they themselves report while a trip is active; school administrators see the records their role is permitted to see. A learner's health profile (allergies, conditions and the like) can be read by the school's staff, so that, for example, a teacher knows about a nut allergy, and by the learner and their guardians. The sick-bay's visit and medication records are limited to the staff the school has given medical access, and to the learner's own guardians.

Messages are visible to the people in the conversation, and the school's administrators can read the conversations in their school. A sent message cannot be edited or removed. Figbloom staff can access data only as needed to operate and support the Service, under the school's instruction.

Fingerprint unlock

The apps can be locked with your phone's fingerprint, if you switch it on in Account. The check is done by your phone's own biometric prompt: the app is told only "yes" or "no". It never receives, stores or sends your fingerprint, and the setting stays on your phone.

Children's data

Because this is a school platform, it necessarily holds data about children. That data is provided and controlled by the school, not collected by Figbloom directly from children. Learners use the Service only with an account their school has given them. Parents/guardians should direct requests about their child's data to their child's school in the first instance; the school can in turn ask us to act on that request.

Data retention

We retain data for as long as the school's account with Figbloom is active, plus a reasonable period after a school leaves the Service in case the school requests an export. A school can request deletion of its data at any time, subject to any academic-record retention requirements that apply to it. Learners who leave are archived by the school rather than erased, so their records stay with the school.

Your rights

Depending on where you are, you may have rights to access, correct or request deletion of your personal information, and to object to how it is used, including under Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019. Requests should go to your school first, since the school controls its own records; the school can escalate to us if needed. You can also contact us directly and we will route the request to the right school. Our page on deleting your account and data explains how, and what a school may have to keep.

If you are unhappy with how a request was handled, you can complain to Kenya's Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (odpc.go.ke).

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections, and each school's data is separated from every other school's by access rules enforced in the database. No system is perfectly secure, and we work to improve these protections over time.
