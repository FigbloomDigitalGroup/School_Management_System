## Full description (Play Store, 4000 chars max)

Figbloom Teacher is the mobile companion to the Figbloom school-management
console, built for the classroom.

- Mark attendance for your classes and sections
- Enter and publish grades — percentage, letter-grade, GPA or CBE
  strand-rubric, matching whatever grading scheme your school uses
- See your timetable and class list at a glance
- Message parents directly and read school notices
- Submit leave requests
- Check your assigned school buses

Figbloom Teacher is provided to you by your school. You'll need a
school-issued account to sign in.
