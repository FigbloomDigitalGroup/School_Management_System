# Play Store / App Store listing copy: Figbloom Schools

One app for everyone at a school (`com.figbloom.schools`): parents, students,
drivers, teachers, and the school's admins and office staff. There was a
separate Teacher app until it was merged in on 2026-11-06; it was never
published, so this is the only listing.

Figbloom Schools

## Full description (Play Store, 4000 chars max)

Figbloom Schools connects a school's families, students, drivers, teachers and
office to each other, in one app. You sign in once and see the screens that
suit your role.

**For parents**
- See your child's attendance, timetable and grades, including CBE
  strand-rubric results, not just a percentage score
- Pay school fees from the app with M-Pesa, or send a payment slip
- Message your child's teacher, call the school, and read school notices
- Track your child's school bus live while it's on a trip
- Switch between children if you have more than one at the school

**For students**
- Check today's timetable and homework
- See your results and teacher feedback
- Read school notices

**For drivers**
- Start and end trips with one tap
- Location shares while a trip is active, even if your phone is locked, so
  parents and school staff can see where the bus is
- Works offline: GPS pings queue on the device and send once you're back in
  signal range

**For teachers**
- Take attendance for your classes and enter and publish marks, in whatever
  grading scheme your school uses (percentage, letter grade, GPA or CBE rubric)
- See your timetable and class list, set homework and share learning material
- Message parents and read school notices
- Ask for leave, and follow it to approval

**For school admins and office staff**
- The day at a glance: attendance by class, fees collected, and what needs you
- Send an announcement to the whole school, a year group or a class
- Approve leave, and arrange cover for the teachers who are away
- Fees: record a payment, and confirm or reject the payment proof families send
- Look a learner up, see their allergies and conditions, and call a parent
- The sick bay: who is in, and doses that are overdue
- See where the school buses are, and acknowledge alerts
- Read the school's chats and the call log

Admin screens are behind a fingerprint lock. Setting the school up (terms,
classes, fee items, timetables) is done on the web console, with the same login.

Figbloom Schools is provided to you by your school. You'll need an account set
up by your school to sign in.
