## Full description (Play Store, 4000 chars max)

Figbloom Schools connects parents, students and school bus drivers to their
child's school, in one app.

**For parents**
- See your child's attendance, timetable and grades — including CBE
  strand-rubric results, not just a percentage score
- Pay school fees directly from the app via M-Pesa
- Message your child's teacher and read school notices
- Track your child's school bus live while it's on a trip
- Switch between children if you have more than one at the school

**For students**
- Check today's timetable and homework
- See your results and teacher feedback
- Read school notices

**For drivers**
- Start and end trips with one tap
- Location shares automatically while a trip is active, even if your phone
  is locked, so parents and school staff can see where the bus is
- Works offline — GPS pings queue on the device and send once you're back
  in signal range

Figbloom Schools is provided to you by your child's school. You'll need an
account set up by your school to sign in.
